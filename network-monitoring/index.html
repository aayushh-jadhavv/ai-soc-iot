Each vehicle is one observed packet and drives along the real direction: source device road → gateway → destination road (another device, or the Internet road). The label on a vehicle is the website/domain. Vehicle size is a visual category, not packet size. EVERY device gets its own road (three road lengths keep many devices apart). Pick a device in the list / filter / by clicking its building: the camera flies to its road and only that device's packets are shown driving.
